# Collaboration Center research

| Source type | Specific pain points | Frequency |
|---|---|---:|
| Reddit/community | Teams lack proven integration knowledge, need client-safe QA, lower cost, structured outputs, and simpler migration. | 5 |
| GitHub issues | Duplicate SSO key generation, hidden generated keys, key creation on every login, authorization bypass risks, and need for scoped roles. | 5 |
| Reviews | Poor documentation, missing/limited features, complexity, alert issues, and manual third-party updates. | 5 |
| Hacker News/community launches | Need per-client/team attribution, inspectable workflows, lower governance cost, identity accountability, and policy-gated approvals. | 5 |
| Stack Overflow/security practice | Automated rotation is often missing, service accounts need lifecycle automation, access ownership must be recorded, stale keys must be revoked. | 4 |
| Competitor changelogs | Per-member budgets, active-key caps, project-scoped developers, member analytics, roles, invites, and organization rollups. | 7 |
| Trend reports | Enterprises need one registry, policy-first enforcement, role controls, resource limits, audit trails, and consistent standards across teams. | 6 |

## RICE
Project RBAC 12.8; member budgets 11.4; key lifecycle 10.8; one-time invitations 8.1; delegated approvals 6.3. All five were implemented.
